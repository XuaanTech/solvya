**Calculating the difference between two dates** is something we all need at some point: knowing how many days are left until an event, how much time has passed since a milestone or meeting a contractual deadline. Here is **how to do it**, the different ways to count, and how to use our tool.

## Calendar days vs business days

| Day type | What it counts | Example (1 Jan → 10 Jan) |
|----------|----------------|--------------------------|
| **Calendar days** | Every day on the calendar | 9 days |
| **Business days** | Monday to Friday only | 6 days (both inclusive) |

The choice depends on **what** you need the calculation for:

- **Calendar days**: contractual deadlines, pregnancy timelines, vacation periods, trial periods.
- **Business days**: project delivery dates, court deadlines, work schedules.

## The most frequent confusion: are both days included?

> From **1 March** to **3 March** there are **2 elapsed days** but **3 days if both are inclusive**.

Our tool uses the usual **elapsed days** criterion for the total, and **inclusive** for business days — the most common in project planning.

| From | To | Elapsed days | Business days (inclusive) |
|------|----|--------------|---------------------------|
| 1 Mar | 3 Mar | 2 | 3 (Mon, Tue, Wed) |
| 1 Mar | 7 Mar | 6 | 5 (Mon–Fri) |
| 1 Mar | 8 Mar | 7 | 5 (Mon–Fri; Sat and Sun excluded) |
| 2 Mar (Mon) | 6 Mar (Fri) | 4 | 5 |

## When do you need a date difference?

- **Projects**: calculate delivery deadlines, sprints and milestones.
- **Education**: days remaining until exams, study periods.
- **Pregnancy**: weeks of gestation, estimated due date (280 days ≈ 40 weeks).
- **Vacations**: remaining rest days, trip planning.
- **Contracts**: delivery deadlines, trial periods, legal expirations.
- **Fitness**: days since starting a routine, challenge tracking.
- **Anniversaries and events**: days to or from a meaningful date.

## Approximate mental calculation

To estimate without a tool:

- **Weeks** = total days ÷ 7 (about 4 weeks and 2 days per month).
- **Months** ≈ total days ÷ 30.
- **Business days** ≈ calendar days × 5/7 (≈ 71 %).

Quick example: a 30-calendar-day deadline ≈ **21 business days** (excluding holidays).

## Difference with legal deadlines (inclusive counting)

In many legal contexts (response deadlines, statutes of limitations), **both days are counted inclusive**, meaning 1 is added to the elapsed-days result. Always verify the counting rule of your jurisdiction.

## Conclusion

- **Calendar days**: every day on the calendar (suitable for general deadlines).
- **Business days**: Monday to Friday, both inclusive (suitable for projects and contracts).
- Holidays: the tool **does not deduct them**; add them manually if needed.
- For legal deadlines, confirm whether both days are counted inclusive.
