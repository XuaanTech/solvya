Converting **Celsius to Fahrenheit** is the most common temperature conversion (travel to the US, Anglo recipes, international news). Here you have the **exact formula**, a **ready-to-print table** and a **mental trick** to estimate without a calculator.

## Exact formula

> **°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32**  
> **°F = °C × 1.8 + 32**  (decimal form)

**Examples:**
- 0 °C → 0 × 1.8 + 32 = **32 °F**
- 20 °C → 20 × 1.8 + 32 = **68 °F**
- 37 °C → 37 × 1.8 + 32 = **98.6 °F**
- 100 °C → 100 × 1.8 + 32 = **212 °F**

## Mental trick: "Double and add 30"

> **°F ≈ °C × 2 + 30**

| °C | Trick | Exact | Error |
|----|-------|-------|-------|
| 0 | 30 | 32 | -2 |
| 10 | 50 | 50 | 0 |
| 20 | 70 | 68 | +2 |
| 25 | 80 | 77 | +3 |
| 30 | 90 | 86 | +4 |
| 35 | 100 | 95 | +5 |

**Validity**: error < 2 °F between 5 and 25 °C (comfort range). Outside that, use the exact formula or the [temperature converter](/herramienta/unidades/).

## Inverse: Fahrenheit to Celsius

> **°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9**  
> **°C = (°F − 32) / 1.8**

Mental inverse trick: **subtract 30 and divide by 2** (°C ≈ (°F − 30) / 2). Same validity range.

## Kelvin (bonus)

> **K = °C + 273.15**  
> **°C = K − 273.15**

Absolute zero (0 K) = −273.15 °C = −459.67 °F.
