Converting **kilometers to miles** is essential when driving in the US, UK or reading Anglo car, plane or boat specs. Here you have the **exact formula**, **reference table**, **mental trick** and the key distinction with the **nautical mile**.

## Exact formula

> **1 km = 0.62137119223733 miles (statute)**  
> **1 mile = 1.609344 km** (exact definition since 1959)

**Conversions:**
- **km → mi**: `miles = km × 0.621371`
- **mi → km**: `km = miles × 1.609344`

## Mental trick: "Divide by 1.6"

> **mi ≈ km / 1.6**  (equivalently: **mi ≈ km × 0.625**)

| km | Trick | Exact | Error |
|----|-------|-------|-------|
| 50 | 31.25 | 31.07 | +0.6 % |
| 100 | 62.5 | 62.14 | +0.6 % |
| 120 | 75.0 | 74.56 | +0.6 % |
| 200 | 125.0 | 124.27 | +0.6 % |

**Systematic error ~+0.6 %** (slight overestimate). Perfect for driving: you see 120 km/h → ~75 mph mentally.

## Nautical mile (important for sea/air)

| Unit | Exact definition | Use |
|------|------------------|-----|
| **Statute mile** | 1.609344 km | Roads, land maps, US/UK |
| **Nautical mile** | **1.852 km** (exact) | Maritime navigation, aviation, GPS |
| **Knot** | 1 nautical mile / hour | Ship/plane speed |

> **1 nautical mile = 1.15078 statute miles**  
> **1 knot = 1.852 km/h = 1.15078 mph**

**Don't confuse them**: a ship at 20 knots goes 37 km/h, not 32 km/h.

## Conclusion

- **Exact**: km × 0.621371 = miles.
- **Mental (driving)**: km / 1.6 ≈ mph (error +0.6 %).
- **Table**: print the speed limit one for the dashboard.
