Converting **pounds to kilos** (and back) shows up at the gym (dumbbells in lbs), at the airport (baggage limit in lbs), in US recipes and in Anglo medical data. Here you have the **exact formula**, **ready table**, **mental trick** and references for **body weight, luggage and gym**.

## Exact formula

> **1 pound (avoirdupois) = 0.45359237 kg** (exact definition since 1959)  
> **1 kg = 2.2046226218... pounds**

**Conversions:**
- **lbs → kg**: `kilos = pounds × 0.45359237`
- **kg → lbs**: `pounds = kilos × 2.20462262`

## Mental trick: "Divide by 2.2"

> **kg ≈ lbs / 2.2**  (or **kg ≈ lbs × 0.45**)

| Pounds | Trick | Exact | Error |
|--------|-------|-------|-------|
| 100 | 45.45 | 45.36 | +0.2 % |
| 150 | 68.18 | 68.04 | +0.2 % |
| 200 | 90.91 | 90.72 | +0.2 % |
| 220 | 100.0 | 99.79 | +0.2 % |

**Error ~+0.2 %** (negligible for everyday use).  
Inverse mental: **lbs ≈ kg × 2.2** (ex: 70 kg × 2.2 = 154 lbs, actual 154.32).
