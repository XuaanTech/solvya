Converting **liters to gallons** has a trap: **there isn't a single gallon**. The **US gallon (3.785 L)** and the **imperial UK gallon (4.546 L)** differ by **~20 %**. Here you have the **exact formulas**, **tables**, **mental tricks** and references for **tank, fuel economy, drinks and irrigation**.

## The critical difference: US vs UK (Imperial)

| Gallon | Exact definition | Liters | Main use |
|--------|------------------|--------|----------|
| **US liquid gallon** | 231 in³ | **3.785411784 L** | USA, Latin America, Philippines, South Korea |
| **UK imperial gallon** | 4.54609 L (defined) | **4.54609 L** | UK, Ireland, Canada (historical), Anglophone Caribbean |
| **US dry gallon** | 268.8025 in³ | 4.40488 L | Grain/agricultural (rarely used) |

> **1 UK gallon = 1.20095 US gallons** → **~20 % larger**.  
> **Always verify** which gallon your source uses (country, "US gal" / "Imp gal" label).

## Exact formulas

### Liters → Gallons
- **US**: `gal_US = L / 3.785411784`
- **UK**: `gal_UK = L / 4.54609`

### Gallons → Liters
- **US**: `L = gal_US × 3.785411784`
- **UK**: `L = gal_UK × 4.54609`

## Mental tricks

| **UK** | 1 gal ≈ **4.5 L** | gal ≈ L / 4.5 | -1.0 % |

**Inverse:**

## Fuel economy: L/100 km ↔ mpg

| Conversion | Exact formula | Approximation |
