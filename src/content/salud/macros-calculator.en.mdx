## What are macronutrients (macros)?

**Macronutrients** are the three nutrients that provide energy (calories) to your body:

| Macro | Calories/g | Main Function | Typical Sources |
|-------|------------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Carbohydrates** | 4 kcal/g | Quick energy, brain fuel | Rice, pasta, bread, fruit, vegetables, legumes |
| **Protein** | 4 kcal/g | Tissue building/repair, enzymes, hormones | Meat, fish, eggs, dairy, tofu, legumes |
| **Fat** | 9 kcal/g | Dense energy, hormones, vitamin absorption | Olive oil, nuts, avocado, fatty fish |

> **Alcohol** provides 7 kcal/g but is not an essential macronutrient and isn't included in the split.

## How this tool calculates your macros

### 1. Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) — Mifflin-St Jeor

The **Mifflin-St Jeor (1990)** equation is the most validated for general population:

| Sex | Formula |
|-----|---------|
| **Male** | `BMR = 10 × weight(kg) + 6.25 × height(cm) - 5 × age(years) + 5` |
| **Female** | `BMR = 10 × weight(kg) + 6.25 × height(cm) - 5 × age(years) - 161` |

> **Alternative**: Katch-McArdle uses body fat % (more accurate if known), but Mifflin-St Jeor is standard without that data.

### 2. Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)

```
TDEE = BMR × Activity Factor
```

| Level | Factor | Description |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| Sedentary | 1.2 | Little/no exercise, desk job |
| Light | 1.375 | Light exercise 1-3 days/week |
| Moderate | 1.55 | Moderate exercise 3-5 days/week |
| Active | 1.725 | Hard exercise 6-7 days/week |
| Very Active | 1.9 | Training 2×/day, heavy physical job |

### 3. Goal → Target Calories

| Goal | Adjustment on TDEE |
|------|-------------------|
| **Maintain** | TDEE (no change) |
| **Lose weight** | TDEE - 20% (min. -500 kcal) |
| **Gain weight** | TDEE + 15% (max. +300 kcal) |
| **Custom** | TDEE + kcal you specify (+/-) |

> **Note**: A 500 kcal/day deficit ≈ 0.5 kg/week theoretical. Reality varies due to metabolic adaptation, NEAT, adherence, etc.

### 4. Split → Grams per macro

```
g_carbs  = (target_calories × %carbs  / 100) / 4
g_protein = (target_calories × %protein / 100) / 4
g_fat    = (target_calories × %fat    / 100) / 9
```

## Complete Example

**Male, 30 years, 180 cm, 85 kg, moderate activity, goal lose, preset High Protein (40/40/20)**

1. **BMR** = 10×85 + 6.25×180 - 5×30 + 5 = 850 + 1125 - 150 + 5 = **1,830 kcal**
2. **TDEE** = 1,830 × 1.55 = **2,837 kcal**
3. **Goal lose** = 2,837 × 0.8 = **2,269 kcal** (deficit ~568 kcal)
4. **Macros**:
   - Carbs: 2,269 × 0.40 / 4 = **227 g**
   - Protein: 2,269 × 0.40 / 4 = **227 g** (2.67 g/kg — high, good for muscle retention)
   - Fat: 2,269 × 0.20 / 9 = **50 g**
